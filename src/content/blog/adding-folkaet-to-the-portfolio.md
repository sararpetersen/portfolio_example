I've added a new project to the site – [Folkæt for Neurodivergent People](/folkaet). If you've read my [recent internship update](/blog/what-im-actually-working-on-at-folkaet), you've already heard a little about the work behind it. Now there's a project page with a prototype, where you can explore the design for yourselves.

The prototype explores a page for neurodivergent users within Folkæt. It introduces different ways of thinking, communicating and working as strengths within a team, with clear entry points to learn more, find people and build a profile. It's part of the accessibility-focused work I've been doing during my internship, where I've been exploring how people can communicate their preferences and support needs.

What interests me about this project is how much of that comes down to the way information is introduced. Before someone builds a profile or reaches out to other people, the page needs to help them understand what Folkæt offers and where they can go next. Those first steps are part of the accessibility work too: the wording, the structure, and how much someone has to work out for themselves.

Adding it here also gives a more concrete picture of my internship. I've written about the process on the blog, but being able to click through a prototype makes the design easier to understand than a written update alone. It brings some of the work I've been talking about into the portfolio itself.

The project is presented as a Figma prototype, and the wider work is still being iterated on. This is just a look at the current design direction, with room for it to develop as the project moves forward.

You can [visit the Folkæt project page](/folkaet) and open the prototype from there.
